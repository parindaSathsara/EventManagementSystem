# Stage — How to Add an Event (Organizer Guide)

A step-by-step guide for event companies publishing an event in the **Stage**
app. Written for the person creating the event — no technical knowledge needed.

---

## 1. Log in as an event company

1. Open the **Stage** app. It starts on the **Timeline** (no login needed to
   browse).
2. Tap the **"+" button** at the top of the Timeline (between the search and
   refresh icons). You can also use the manager entry at the bottom of the
   **Calendar** tab.
3. Log in with your event-company email and password (provided separately).
4. You land on your organizer **Home** — it shows your **Followers**, the
   **days until your next event**, and your active events.

## 2. Start a new event

Tap **New Event** on Home. The create form opens. Fill it top to bottom:

### ① Event flyer  ← the most important part

This is the artwork guests see on the Timeline and the event page.

- Tap **Upload flyer** to pick a photo from your gallery, or **Take photo**
  to capture a printed flyer with the camera.
- A progress bar shows while it uploads; the preview appears above when done.
- Tap the **trash button** to remove it and pick another.

**Flyer specs:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Best size | **1080 × 1350 px (portrait 4:5)** — Instagram-poster format |
| Also fine | Anything up to 2:3 portrait (e.g. 1080 × 1620) shows in full |
| File | JPG, PNG or WebP, up to **10 MB** |
| Design tip | Put the **event name and date in the TOP half** of the artwork — on the Timeline the flyer is shown from the top down, so the top always stays visible |

If you don't add a flyer, the event shows with a colored background instead
(pick one from the color strip under the buttons).

### ② Event details

- **Title** — e.g. "Lost Garden". Keep it short; it appears in big letters.
- **Description** — what's happening, who's playing, what to expect. Guests
  see the first lines on the Timeline and the full text inside the event.

### ③ Category

Tap one: **Live Concert · EDM · Festival · Hip Hop · Event · Other**.

### ④ Date & time

Everything is picked by tapping chips — no typing:

- **Year → Month → Day**
- **Starts** (hour) and **Start minutes**
- **Ends** (hour) and **End minutes** — start and end minutes are set
  **separately**.
- If the end time is earlier than the start (e.g. 8 PM – 2 AM), the app
  automatically treats it as ending the **next day**.

### ⑤ Venue

- **Venue name** — e.g. "Stardust Arena".
- **City** — e.g. "Colombo". Guests can find your event by searching the city.

### ⑥ Age restriction

Tap one: All ages · 13+ · 18+ · 21+.

### ⑦ Ticketing link (how guests buy)

Paste your **third-party ticketing page URL** (one link per event). When a
guest taps **Buy Now / Buy Tickets**, the app opens this page directly.
Must start with `https://`.

### ⑧ Ticket types

Add each ticket **name + price** (e.g. "General — 4500", "VIP — 8000").

- These are **display-only** for guests — actual sales happen on your
  ticketing page from step ⑦.
- Leave the price empty to show **"Free"**.
- No quantity is needed.
- The **cheapest price** is shown next to the Buy Now button.

### ⑨ Line up

- **Slot #1 is your headline artist** — it stays first (no label is shown to
  guests; the order itself is the billing).
- Tap **Add artist** for each act. Adding their Instagram / Facebook / TikTok
  lets guests tap through to the artist's socials.
- **Surprise act?** Tap **"Revealed Soon"** — guests see a surprise slot with
  "More artists are coming soon". After publishing, edit the event and type
  the real name to reveal them.
- Use the **↑ ↓ arrows** to reorder artists (the headliner stays fixed).

### ⑩ Promotion & socials

Add your company's **Facebook page, Instagram and TikTok** — guests see these
on the event page. (The flyer URL field here is only a fallback — normally
the flyer from step ① fills it automatically.)

### ⑪ Publish

- Keep **Auto-publish** selected and tap **Publish event**.
- Your event immediately appears on the guest **Timeline** and **Calendar**,
  and everyone who follows your company gets a **"New event" alert**.

---

## 3. After publishing — editing your event

You can change a published event any time (reveal surprise artists, adjust
prices, swap the ticket link, change the flyer):

1. On your organizer **Home**, find the event under **Active events** and tap
   the **edit (brush) button** — or go to **My Events → Edit**.
2. Change what you need and tap **Save changes**.
3. Changes go live immediately, **and the event pops back to the top of your
   followers' Alerts** marked **"Updated"** — so reveals and price changes
   get seen again.

## 4. What guests see (for reference)

- **Timeline:** your company name on top, the flyer (top part always visible),
  the first lines of the description, the line-up, and a **Buy Tickets**
  button that opens your ticketing page.
- **Event page:** the full flyer, your company card (with Follow button),
  date/venue, full description, line-up with artist socials, the ticket
  types + prices, and **Buy Now**.
- Once the event's end time passes, the Buy button automatically greys out to
  **"Event ended"** — nothing to do on your side.
- Guests who tap **Follow** on your company are counted on your Home screen
  and get alerted 5 days and 1 day before each of your events.

## Quick checklist before you publish

- [ ] Flyer uploaded (portrait, title in top half)
- [ ] Title + description written
- [ ] Category picked
- [ ] Date, start & end times set (minutes checked separately)
- [ ] Venue + city filled
- [ ] Ticketing link pasted (`https://…`)
- [ ] At least one ticket type with a price
- [ ] Line-up added ("Revealed Soon" for surprises)
- [ ] Socials added
